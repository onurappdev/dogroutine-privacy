Privacy Policy

DogRoutine · Last updated November 5, 2026

Overview

DogRoutine is a dog care routine tracker. This policy describes the data the app keeps on your device and the limited data processed by the services listed below.

Data on Your Device

Dog profiles, routines, care history, health records, weights, training records, notes, the owner name and phone number you may enter, and your dog's photo are stored on your device. DogRoutine has no account system and does not upload this content to our servers. Your device backup settings may include app data.

Third-Party Services

DogRoutine uses TelemetryDeck to understand how the app is used, such as opening the app, moving through setup, creating or completing a routine, opening a feature, and viewing an offer. Signals may include the app version, operating system, device model, device language, the language chosen in the app, your subscription state, counts in fixed ranges (such as the number of routines), the setup choices you make from fixed lists, whether notifications are allowed, and technical usage information. We do not send your dog's name, owner names, phone numbers, free-text notes, photos or report contents to analytics.

We use RevenueCat and Apple to validate purchases and restore Premium access. This involves an app user identifier, purchase and subscription information, and technical information needed to process the transaction. If you installed the app from an Apple Search Ads advertisement, Apple AdServices attribution (such as the campaign, ad group and keyword) is collected through RevenueCat to measure which ads lead to installations and subscriptions. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Installation Identifier

On first launch the app creates a random identifier for this installation. It is not derived from your name, email, Apple Account, phone number or device advertising identifier. It is sent with analytics signals and stored with RevenueCat so that anonymous usage, purchases and ad attribution of the same installation can be analysed together. We do not use it to identify you or to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Deleting and reinstalling the app creates a new identifier.

DogRoutine does not display third-party advertising or request the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Notifications

DogRoutine uses local notifications to remind you of upcoming care. Reminder content is scheduled on your device. Whether notification permission was granted may be included in a usage signal; reminder text is not sent with that signal.

Photos and Exported Files

A photo you select for your dog's profile is stored locally. The app can create a veterinary PDF, a care guide or a backup file when you request one. You choose where to save or share each file. Files you export may contain names, notes and photos, and the destination you choose handles its own copy. DogRoutine does not automatically send these files to a veterinarian or to an analytics service.

Children's Privacy

DogRoutine does not ask for a child's name, age or contact information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, please contact us.

Your Choices

You can turn off notifications in the app or in iPhone Settings. Because analytics and purchase data are tied only to a random installation identifier, we generally cannot link them to you. If you contact us, we will help with any request we are able to fulfil.

Deletion

You can delete your records in the app and remove app data from your device. Copies you exported or included in device backups must be managed separately. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
